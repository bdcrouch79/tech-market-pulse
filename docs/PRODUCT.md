# Product

## Product statement

Tech Market Pulse is a public technology-market intelligence dashboard that helps a visitor quickly understand which major technology assets are leading, where risk is increasing, and how tightly the tracked market is moving together.

## Primary user

A technically curious investor, operator, builder, recruiter, or prospective Crouch Development client who wants a fast, visual market-structure view and a concrete example of Bryan Crouch's applied analytics and product engineering work.

## Primary outcome

Within a few minutes, the visitor should be able to answer:

- What is leading and lagging?
- Is participation broad or narrow?
- Where is realized volatility highest?
- Which assets have experienced the deepest drawdowns?
- Which names are outperforming QQQ recently?
- Is the technology complex moving as one trade or dispersing?

## Product boundaries

Tech Market Pulse is:

- descriptive market analytics
- a public Crouch Development technical showcase
- explainable and methodology-driven
- intentionally focused on a small fixed technology universe

Tech Market Pulse is not:

- a brokerage or trading interface
- personalized investment advice
- a recommendation engine
- a forecasting model
- a portfolio optimizer
- an intraday trading system

## Success criteria

The application is successful as a showcase when it is visually strong, loads useful market context without requiring authentication, clearly explains its math, degrades safely when live data is unavailable, and demonstrates a coherent data-to-product pipeline.
